Campus Complaint Mangement System
The Campus Complaint Management System is a web-based application that allows students to submit complaints about problems within the college/campus and allows administrators to manage those complaints until they are resolved.

The main objective is:

To digitize the complaint process and provide a centralized system for submitting, tracking, managing, and resolving campus complaints.

Instead of students manually approaching administrators, writing applications, or repeatedly asking about complaint status, everything is maintained in one system.

The Campus Complaint Management System provides services such as student authentication, complaint submission, AI-based complaint summarization, priority prediction, automatic department and staff assignment, staff response management, intelligent status updates, complaint tracking, administrator monitoring, notifications, and AI decision logging. These services integrate full-stack development, secure authentication, centralized database management, and AI-based automation into a unified complaint resolution workflow.
